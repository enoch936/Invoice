LINEAR EQUATION SYSTEMS

    Exact rational arithmetic — fractions like 8/5 stay exact. Rows are eliminated with recorded row operations; consistency is decided by comparing rank(A) with rank([A|b]).

    POLYNOMIAL ROOT FINDER

      Degree 1–2 results are exact (with discriminant). Higher degrees: square-free reduction (gcd with the derivative) then the Durand–Kerner iterative method — every root is verified against |p(r)|. Real coefficients only.

      COMPLEX NUMBER CALCULATOR

      Square root, logarithm and powers use the principal complex branch. The Argand diagram shows z (solid), its conjugate (dashed) and the |z| circle.

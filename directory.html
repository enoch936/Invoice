How the directory works

Every tool links to a real, standalone page with its method, a worked example and its assumptions, so you can check the maths or do it by hand. The search box, category buttons, type filter, sort control and view switcher all work in your browser, with no account and no tracking of what you type.

Counts are read from the same registry that builds this page, so nothing here can fall out of date.

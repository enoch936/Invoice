How the directory works

Every calculator is a standalone page with its formula, a worked example and its assumptions, so you can check the maths or do it by hand. Use the search box or the category buttons to narrow the list — filtering happens in your browser, with no account and no tracking of what you type.

If a tool you need is missing, the catalog is designed to grow: new calculators are added as real, tested pages rather than placeholders.

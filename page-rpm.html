Page RPM calculator (revenue per 1,000 pageviews)

Page RPM is the money you make for every 1,000 pageviews. Enter revenue and pageviews to see how your site is performing.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

RPM = (Revenue / Pageviews) × 1,000

Worked example

  1. A site earns $500 in a month from 100,000 pageviews.
  2. RPM = (500 / 100,000) × 1,000 = $5.00.
  3. Revenue per pageview = 500 / 100,000 = $0.005.

Assumptions and limitations

  • RPM is an estimate from the revenue and pageviews you supply; real RPMs swing with traffic source, season and niche.
  • It assumes all pageviews are valid and serve ads — no unfilled inventory or bot filtering is deducted.
  • No income is guaranteed and network revenue shares are not modelled here.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good page RPM?

It varies hugely by niche and country — display entertainment sites often sit in the low single digits, while finance and insurance traffic can reach tens of dollars.

How is page RPM different from session RPM?

Page RPM uses pageviews; session RPM uses sessions. If readers view several pages per session, page RPM is the lower of the two.

Why do RPMs differ by country?

Advertisers pay more in higher-spending markets, so traffic from those countries typically earns a higher RPM.

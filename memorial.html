Frequently asked questions

Is my text stored anywhere?

No. The name, dates and message live only in the link you share. PARCHXA has no server or database for them.

Is the candle counter shared between visitors?

No. It counts candles lit on your own device and is stored in your browser.

Why was my text rejected?

Pages are checked for offensive language and unsafe characters. Remove the flagged words and try again.

Does the page play sound?

No. This version has no audio.

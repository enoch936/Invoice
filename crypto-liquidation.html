TRADING, FOREX & CRYPTO CALCULATOR

Crypto liquidation price calculator

Leverage magnifies both gains and losses. Enter the entry price, leverage and maintenance rate to see where an isolated position is estimated to liquidate.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Long: L = entry × (1 − 1/leverage + m). Short: L = entry × (1 + 1/leverage − m)

Worked example

  1. Long 10× at $100 with a 0.5% maintenance margin rate.
  2. Liquidation = 100 × (1 − 0.10 + 0.005) = $90.50.
  3. That is a 9.5% fall; at 20× it would be 100 × (1 − 0.05 + 0.005) = $95.50, only 4.5% away.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Isolated margin with a fixed maintenance margin rate; cross margin and tiered maintenance rates are not modelled.
  • Funding payments, fees, slippage and any margin added or removed while the trade is open are ignored.
  • Exchanges use a mark price and their own rules, so the real trigger price will differ from this estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the liquidation price not just the leverage?

The exchange keeps a maintenance margin to cover the position, so liquidation happens a little closer than the pure 1/leverage point.

Is a liquidation avoidable?

Adding margin or reducing the position can push the liquidation price further away, but leverage always keeps the risk of a total loss for the margin used.

Does this work for shorts?

Yes. Choose short and the formula flips, so liquidation sits above the entry price.

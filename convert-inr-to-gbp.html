Convert Indian rupee (INR) to British pound (GBP)

The Indian rupee (₹) is the currency of India, issued under the authority of the Reserve Bank of India. The British pound (£) is used in the United Kingdom and is managed by the Bank of England. To convert, multiply the INR amount by the current INR/GBP rate. The table above shows common amounts at the latest rate we could load.

If you invoice clients in GBP but think in INR, convert the total on the day you send the invoice and state the rate used. Prices quoted in one currency and paid in another can shift between invoice date and payment date, so many freelancers add a short validity period or a currency-adjustment clause.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert INR to GBP?

Multiply the INR amount by the INR to GBP exchange rate. Enter the amount above and the page does it using the latest rate it can load.

What is the INR to GBP rate today?

The page loads an indicative mid-market rate when it can and shows it above the result. Rates change throughout the day.

Why is my bank's INR to GBP rate different?

Banks and transfer services add a margin to the mid-market rate and may charge fixed fees, so the amount you receive is usually lower than a pure conversion.

Can I use my own INR/GBP rate?

Yes. Type a rate in the manual override box and the result uses it instead.

Which currency should I invoice in, INR or GBP?

Invoice in the currency your client pays in and your costs are in, if you can. If they differ, state the exchange rate and date on the invoice.

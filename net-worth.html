Net worth calculator

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. Track it over time to see whether your finances are moving forward.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Net worth = Total assets − Total liabilities

Worked example

  1. Assets $50,000, liabilities $20,000.
  2. Net worth = $30,000.
  3. Debt is 40% of assets, so there is a healthy buffer.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Assets are entered at a realistic current value.
  • Included only what you could convert or owe.
  • Not a valuation of pensions you cannot access.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as an asset?

Anything with resale value you own: cash, deposits, investments, property, vehicles and valuable items.

Should I include my car?

Include it at its current resale value, and only include the loan in liabilities — never the full purchase price.

Customer acquisition cost calculator

CAC is what it costs, on average, to win one new customer. Compare it with lifetime value to see whether growth is sustainable.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

CAC = Sales & marketing spend / New customers

Worked example

  1. Spend $10,000 on marketing and win 200 new customers.
  2. CAC = 10000 / 200 = $50 per customer.
  3. If each customer is worth $300 of gross profit, the payback is comfortable.

Assumptions and limitations

  • All sales and marketing spend is included in the period.
  • Customers are attributed correctly to that spend.
  • No allowance for organic or referred customers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good CAC?

It depends entirely on value earned per customer. A common rule is to keep LTV at least three times CAC.

Should CAC include salaries?

Fully-loaded CAC includes marketing spend plus the salaries of the people doing sales and marketing.

Why did CAC rise?

Channels saturate, competition bids up ad prices, or targeting got looser — check each channel separately.

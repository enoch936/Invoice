How much can I borrow? (loan affordability)

Lenders cap how much of your income can go to debt. Enter your income and a target ratio to estimate your borrowing ceiling.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Max loan = PMT × (1 − (1 + r)^−n) / r

Worked example

  1. Income $6,000, debts $500, target 36%, 6% over 30 years.
  2. Max payment = 0.36 × 6000 − 500 = $1,660.
  3. That supports a loan of about $276,800.

Assumptions and limitations

  • A target debt-to-income ratio stands in for a lender's own rules.
  • Rates and income are fixed.
  • Taxes, insurance and fees are excluded.

Frequently asked questions

Is the borrowing ceiling a promise?

No. It is an estimate from your chosen ratio; real lenders also weigh credit history, deposits and policy.

What ratio should I use?

Many lenders prefer total debt below 36% of gross income, with housing under 28%.

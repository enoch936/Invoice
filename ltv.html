Customer lifetime value calculator

Lifetime value estimates the gross profit a customer brings before they leave. It uses your monthly churn rate to work out the average lifetime.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

LTV = ARPU × Gross margin % / Monthly churn rate

Worked example

  1. ARPU $50 a month, 80% gross margin, 5% monthly churn.
  2. Average lifetime = 1 / 0.05 = 20 months.
  3. LTV = ($50 × 0.80) × 20 = $800 of gross profit.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Churn and ARPU are constant over the customer's life.
  • LTV is margin-adjusted — it measures gross profit, not revenue.
  • No discounting of future cash flows is applied.

Frequently asked questions

Why use margin-adjusted LTV?

Revenue alone overstates value — only the gross profit left after serving the customer can pay for acquisition and overheads.

How does churn affect LTV?

LTV is inversely proportional to churn. Halving churn doubles the average lifetime and the lifetime value.

What LTV:CAC ratio should I target?

A ratio of about 3:1 is a common benchmark — enough margin to grow while staying efficient.

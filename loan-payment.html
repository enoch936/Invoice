Loan payment calculator

Enter the amount, interest rate and term to see the level monthly payment and what the loan really costs in interest.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

PMT = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)^−n)

Worked example

  1. P = $20,000, r = 8%/12 = 0.0066667, n = 60.
  2. PMT = 20000 × 0.0066667 / (1 − 1.0066667^−60) = $405.53.
  3. Total paid = 60 × $405.53 = $24,332, so interest is about $4,332.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Payments are level and made at the end of each month.
  • The rate is fixed for the whole term.
  • No arrangement fees or insurance are included.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the interest front-loaded?

Interest is charged on the outstanding balance, which is largest at the start, so early payments are mostly interest.

What if I pay extra?

Extra principal payments shorten the term and cut total interest. Use the loan payoff calculator to model that.

Does this include fees?

No. Add arrangement fees to the amount or compare APRs to include them.

BMI calculator (body mass index)

BMI is a quick screening ratio of weight to height. It is a rough population measure, not a diagnosis.

Formula

BMI = weight(kg) / height(m)²

Worked example

  1. 70 kg and 175 cm.
  2. Height in metres = 1.75 m; squared = 3.0625.
  3. BMI = 70 / 3.0625 = 22.9, in the healthy range.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Uses the World Health Organization adult cut-offs (18.5, 25, 30).
  • BMI does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not reliable for athletes, children or pregnancy.
  • General population estimate only — not medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI a diagnosis?

No. It is a screening tool for populations. A clinician looks at body composition, history and other measurements.

Why might BMI be misleading?

Muscular people can have a high BMI while carrying little fat, and older adults can have a ‘healthy’ BMI with low muscle.

Are the cut-offs the same for everyone?

No. Different thresholds are used for children and some populations; this tool uses the WHO adult scale.

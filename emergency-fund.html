FINANCIAL CALCULATOR

Emergency fund calculator

An emergency fund covers essential spending if income stops. See a target for 3, 6 and 12 months, and how long it takes to save.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Target = Essential monthly expenses × Months of cover

Worked example

  1. $2,500 of essential spending a month, 6 months of cover, $1,000 saved, saving $400/mo.
  2. Target = $15,000; remaining = $14,000.
  3. That takes 35 months at $400 a month.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Expenses are your essentials, not your full lifestyle.
  • No interest is modelled (keep this money liquid).
  • Savings are regular.

Frequently asked questions

How many months do I need?

Three months for stable salaried work; six or more for freelancers, single-income households or volatile sectors.

Where should I keep it?

Somewhere safe and instantly accessible — a separate savings account rather than investments you might have to sell at a loss.

Ratio calculator

A ratio compares two quantities. Enter two positive numbers to reduce them to their simplest form and see how the total splits.

Formula

a : b = (a ÷ gcd) : (b ÷ gcd) · A share = a / (a + b) × 100

Worked example

  1. Ratio 4 : 6, and gcd(4, 6) = 2.
  2. Divide both sides: 4/2 : 6/2 = 2 : 3.
  3. Total parts = 10, so A is 40% and B is 60%.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Both values must be positive; ratios of zero or negatives are not reduced here.
  • The ratio is scaled by the greatest common divisor, so the proportions between the two numbers never change.

Frequently asked questions

What does simplifying a ratio do?

It divides both sides by their greatest common divisor so the numbers are as small as possible while keeping the same proportion.

How do I share an amount using this ratio?

Read the percentages: for 4:6, give 40% of the amount to A and 60% to B.

What if the numbers are decimals?

The tool still finds a common divisor, so 1.5 : 2.5 simplifies to 3 : 5.

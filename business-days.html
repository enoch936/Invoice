Business days calculator

Work out how many Monday–Friday days fall between two dates, minus any public holidays you need to exclude.

Formula

working days = weekdays(Mon–Fri) − public holidays

Worked example

  1. From 2026-01-01 to 2026-01-31.
  2. There are 22 Monday–Friday days in that span.
  3. Excluding one public holiday leaves 21 working days.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The working week is Monday–Friday.
  • You enter the number of public holidays yourself, since holiday calendars differ by country.
  • The start day is counted and the end day is not.

Frequently asked questions

Does it know my country's holidays?

No — holiday calendars vary and change, so you enter the count. The weekday count itself is exact.

Can the result be negative?

If you exclude more holidays than there are weekdays, the count goes negative, which warns you to check the numbers.

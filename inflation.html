FINANCIAL CALCULATOR

Inflation and purchasing-power calculator

Inflation erodes purchasing power. See what a future cost looks like and what today's money will buy later.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Future cost = Amount × (1 + i)^t · Real value = Amount / (1 + i)^t

Worked example

  1. $1,000 at 3% inflation for 10 years.
  2. Factor = 1.03^10 = 1.3439.
  3. The same basket costs $1,343.92; today's $1,000 will only buy $743.99 of it.

Assumptions and limitations

  • A single constant inflation rate.
  • The same basket of goods throughout.
  • No change in taxes or interest rates.

Frequently asked questions

Why does cash lose value?

If prices rise faster than the interest cash earns, the same amount of cash buys less each year.

Is my country's inflation the same?

No. Enter the rate that matches your currency and economy — official statistics publish monthly figures.

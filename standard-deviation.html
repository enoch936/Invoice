Standard deviation calculator

Standard deviation says how far values typically sit from the mean. Choose sample or population to match how your data was collected.

Formula

sample s = √( Σ(x − x̄)² / (n − 1) ) · population σ = √( Σ(x − x̄)² / n )

Worked example

  1. Sample data: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9 with a mean of 5.
  2. The squared deviations sum to 32 across 8 values.
  3. Population variance = 32/8 = 4, so σ = 2; sample variance = 32/7 ≈ 4.571, so s ≈ 2.138.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The sample formula divides by n − 1 for an unbiased estimate; the population formula divides by n.
  • Values are treated as a single flat data set; no grouping or weighting is applied.

Frequently asked questions

Sample or population?

Use sample when your numbers are a subset used to estimate a wider group; use population when you have every value in the group.

Why divide by n − 1?

Using n − 1 corrects the tendency of a sample to underestimate the true spread of the population.

What does a small standard deviation mean?

Values cluster tightly around the mean. A large one means they are widely spread.

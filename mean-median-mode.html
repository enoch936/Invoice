Mean, median and mode calculator

Mean, median and mode are three different ways to describe the centre of a data set. Together they reveal whether values cluster or skew.

Formula

mean = Σx / n · median = middle value of the sorted list · mode = most frequent value

Worked example

  1. Numbers: 2, 4, 4, 4, 5, 5, 7, 9.
  2. Sum = 40 over 8 values, so the mean is 5.
  3. Median = (4 + 5) / 2 = 4.5; mode = 4 (it appears three times); range = 9 − 2 = 7.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The mode is reported only when a value actually repeats; otherwise the result is 'no mode'.
  • For an even count the median is the average of the two middle values.

Frequently asked questions

When is the median better than the mean?

When the data is skewed or has outliers — for example house prices or incomes — the median describes a typical value more fairly.

Can there be more than one mode?

Yes. A data set can be bimodal or multimodal; this tool reports the single most frequent value.

What does the range tell me?

It is the distance between the largest and smallest values, a quick if crude measure of how spread out the data is.

TRADING, FOREX & CRYPTO CALCULATOR

Crypto trading fees calculator

Fees are easy to ignore trade by trade and expensive over a year. Enter your trade size, fee rate and number of trades to see the true cost.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Total fees = trade amount × fee% × number of trades

Worked example

  1. 20 trades of $5,000 at a 0.1% fee.
  2. Fee per trade = 5,000 × 0.1% = $5.00.
  3. Total = 5 × 20 = $100 on $100,000 of volume, an effective cost of 0.1%.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Every trade is the same size and pays the same fee rate.
  • A round trip (a buy and a sell) counts as two trades if your exchange charges both sides.
  • Spreads, slippage, funding and withdrawal fees are excluded; tiered or maker/taker schedules may change the rate over time.

Frequently asked questions

Why do fees matter so much?

They are paid on every trade whether it wins or loses. Many small trades can quietly cost more than the profit a strategy earns.

What is a maker versus taker fee?

A taker fee applies when your order fills immediately against the book; a maker fee is usually lower and applies when you add liquidity with a resting limit order.

Should I include spreads?

Yes, ideally. The spread is an extra cost on every fill; this calculator covers only the explicit fee you enter.

    How it works

    EMA = price × k + previous EMA × (1 − k), with k = 2 ÷ (period + 1). RSI uses Wilder smoothing of average gains and losses over 14 bars. A buy signal appears when the fast EMA crosses above the slow EMA while RSI is not above your limit; the position closes when it crosses back down.

    Candles come from the Binance public market-data API directly in your browser. If it is unreachable, the chart reports the error and offers a retry — no substitute or simulated prices are ever shown.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is this chart data live?

    When your network can reach the Binance public API, yes. Turn on Live to stream the current candle. Data can be delayed or blocked in some regions; if the feed is unreachable the chart shows an error and a retry button rather than substitute data.

    Does the backtest include fees?

    Yes. Fee and slippage are charged on both entry and exit, and you can change them. Real costs vary by exchange, size and volatility.

    What does the confluence score mean?

    It counts how many of four simple rules agree on the latest candle: trend, price versus the slow EMA, an RSI zone and recent momentum. It describes the present, it does not forecast.

    Why can a backtest mislead me?

    Fitting rules to past prices (overfitting), testing only assets that survived (survivorship bias) and using information not available at the time (look-ahead bias) all inflate results.

    Can I trade from this page?

    No. It is an educational charting and testing tool with no trading or account features.

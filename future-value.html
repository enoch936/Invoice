Future value calculator

Future value takes a present amount and projects what it becomes once interest or growth is applied for a number of years.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

FV = PV × (1 + r/n)^(n t)

Worked example

  1. PV = $10,000, r = 7%, t = 10 years, annual compounding.
  2. FV = 10000 × 1.07^10 = $19,671.51.
  3. Switch to monthly compounding and the same inputs give 10000 × (1 + 0.07/12)^120 ≈ $20,097.

Assumptions and limitations

  • A constant rate.
  • No contributions or withdrawals.
  • Compounding is regular.

Frequently asked questions

How is this different from compound interest?

They are the same idea. This calculator is the pure lump-sum case without regular contributions.

Can the rate be negative?

Yes — a negative rate models deflation or a loss and gives a future value below the present value.

Break-even calculator

Break-even is the sales volume where total revenue exactly covers total costs. Sell more and you make a profit; sell less and you make a loss.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Break-even units = Fixed costs / (Price − Variable cost per unit)

Worked example

  1. Fixed costs $10,000, price $50, variable cost $30 per unit.
  2. Contribution = $50 − $30 = $20 per unit.
  3. Units = 10000 / 20 = 500 units; revenue = 500 × $50 = $25,000.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fixed costs, price and variable cost are constant over the range of sales.
  • Everything produced is sold.
  • No volume discounts or step changes in cost are modelled.

Frequently asked questions

Why round break-even units up?

You cannot sell a fraction of a unit and still cover costs, so the practical target is the next whole unit.

What if price is below variable cost?

Each sale then loses money, so break-even is impossible — raise the price or cut unit costs.

How do I lower my break-even point?

Increase the contribution per unit (higher price or cheaper variable costs) or reduce fixed costs.

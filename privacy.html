Privacy notice

Last updated 12 October 2026. A plain-language summary of what this toolkit does — and does not do — with your information.

What stays in your browser

Invoices, quotations, drafts, and preferences you create are saved in your browser's local storage. This data stays on your own device. It is not uploaded to us, and we cannot see it.

  • Drafts are tied to the browser and device where you created them.
  • Clearing your browser data, or using private/incognito mode, may remove them.
  • Data does not sync across devices or browsers.

What we do not collect

This static toolkit has no accounts, no login, no server-side database, and no payment processing. We do not collect names, email addresses, client details, or document contents. We do not use advertising or behavioral tracking, and we do not sell data.

Third-party requests

The pages load web fonts from Google Fonts when online. That request is made by your browser directly to Google and is subject to Google's own privacy policy. No document content is included in that request. If you prefer, you can block it — the tools still work with fallback fonts.

Your responsibilities

Because drafts are stored locally, you are responsible for keeping backups and for protecting any shared or public device. Do not enter passwords, payment-card numbers, or other sensitive information into these tools.

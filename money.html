How it works

Compound growth applies the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12) to the balance, then adds your contribution each month.

Retainer value = fee ÷ included hours. CAGR = (end ÷ start)^(1 ÷ years) − 1.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a freelancer set aside for taxes?

It depends on your country, income and deductions. Many freelancers reserve a flat percentage of each payment, often between 20% and 35%, then confirm the right figure with a local accountant.

Should I pay tax quarterly?

Some countries require estimated quarterly payments for self-employed people. The calculator shows a quarterly amount so you can plan, but check your local rules and deadlines.

How does compound interest work?

Interest is earned on both your original money and on previously earned interest. Regular monthly contributions make the effect larger over longer periods.

Is a retainer better than hourly billing?

A retainer gives predictable income, but it pays less per hour if the client uses all included hours and your hourly rate is higher. Compare the effective hourly rate to your normal rate.

What is CAGR?

Compound annual growth rate is the steady yearly growth that would turn a starting value into an ending value over a period. It smooths out year-to-year swings.

Convert Indian rupee (INR) to South African rand (ZAR)

The Indian rupee (₹) is the currency of India, issued under the authority of the Reserve Bank of India. The South African rand (R) is used in South Africa and is managed by the South African Reserve Bank. To convert, multiply the INR amount by the current INR/ZAR rate. The table above shows common amounts at the latest rate we could load.

If you invoice clients in ZAR but think in INR, convert the total on the day you send the invoice and state the rate used. Prices quoted in one currency and paid in another can shift between invoice date and payment date, so many freelancers add a short validity period or a currency-adjustment clause.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert INR to ZAR?

Multiply the INR amount by the INR to ZAR exchange rate. Enter the amount above and the page does it using the latest rate it can load.

What is the INR to ZAR rate today?

The page loads an indicative mid-market rate when it can and shows it above the result. Rates change throughout the day.

Why is my bank's INR to ZAR rate different?

Banks and transfer services add a margin to the mid-market rate and may charge fixed fees, so the amount you receive is usually lower than a pure conversion.

Can I use my own INR/ZAR rate?

Yes. Type a rate in the manual override box and the result uses it instead.

Which currency should I invoice in, INR or ZAR?

Invoice in the currency your client pays in and your costs are in, if you can. If they differ, state the exchange rate and date on the invoice.

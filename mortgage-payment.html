FINANCIAL CALCULATOR

Mortgage payment calculator

Work out the principal-and-interest payment and the escrow costs (tax, insurance, HOA) that make up your real monthly housing bill.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Total = P&I + (value × tax%)/12 + insurance/12 + HOA

Worked example

  1. $300,000 home, $60,000 down, 6.5% over 30 years, 1.1% tax, $1,200 insurance.
  2. Loan = $240,000; P&I = $1,516.96.
  3. Tax = $275/mo, insurance = $100/mo, so the total is about $1,891.96.

Assumptions and limitations

  • A fixed rate over the full term.
  • Property tax is a flat percent of the purchase price.
  • PMI, closing costs and maintenance are not included.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in 'principal and interest'?

Only the repayment of the loan and the interest on it. Tax, insurance and HOA are escrow or additional costs.

Should I put down more?

A larger down payment lowers the loan, the payment and often avoids mortgage insurance, but ties up cash.

Does this include PMI?

No. If your down payment is under 20%, lenders may add mortgage insurance — add it to the HOA/other field.

WEBSITE & CREATOR INCOME CALCULATOR

Creator hourly rate calculator

Turning income into an hourly figure shows what your time is really worth. Enter the period's revenue and hours worked.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Hourly rate = Revenue ÷ Hours worked

Worked example

  1. You earn $5,000 in a month of creating.
  2. You worked 120 hours across filming, editing and admin.
  3. Hourly rate = 5,000 / 120 = $41.67 per hour.
  4. A 40-hour week at that rate would pay $1,666.67.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Hours should include all unpaid admin and marketing time, not just production, or the rate will be overstated.
  • Revenue is the gross amount you enter — tax, equipment and platform fees are not deducted.
  • The figure is an estimate for a single period; income and hours vary, and no income is guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Why include admin time?

If you only count filming, the hourly rate looks far better than the reality of running a channel, which includes editing, thumbnails and emails.

Should this include affiliate and sponsor income?

Yes — use total revenue from all sources so the hourly rate reflects what the work actually earns.

How can I raise my effective hourly rate?

Either earn more (higher RPM, better sponsorship rates) or work fewer hours by reusing and batching content.

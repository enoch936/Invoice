Debt snowball calculator

The snowball method pays the smallest balance first for quick wins, then rolls the freed-up payment into the next debt.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Pay minimums on all debts, throw the surplus at the smallest, then roll it forward.

Worked example

  1. Card $800 @ 22%, store card $1,500 @ 19%, car $6,000 @ 7%, plus $100 extra.
  2. The $800 card is cleared first, then its payment joins the attack on the $1,500 card.
  3. Each cleared debt accelerates the next.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Minimum payments are enough to cover each month's interest.
  • No new borrowing.
  • Payments are applied at the end of each month.

Frequently asked questions

Snowball or avalanche?

The snowball gives fast psychological wins; the avalanche (highest rate first) usually pays the least interest. Compare both here.

How do I format my debts?

One per line as: name, balance, APR %, minimum payment — for example ‘Card, 800, 22, 40’.

How to use these calculators

Each page computes (sell − buy) × quantity, subtracts estimated round-trip fees and shows the net result, the percentage return and the break-even sell price. There is also a small table showing what happens at several possible sell prices, which is useful for setting targets before you trade.

For risk and position sizing, pair these pages with the trading risk suite and the live chart & backtester.

Frequently asked questions

How do these crypto profit calculators work?

Each page takes your own buy price, sell price and quantity, applies a round-trip fee estimate and returns the net profit or loss in USD, the percentage return and the break-even sell price.

Do they use live prices?

No. Entering your real fill prices gives a more accurate result than any delayed quote, and keeps the page private and offline-friendly.

Which coins are covered?

Bitcoin, Ethereum, Solana, XRP, Cardano, Dogecoin, BNB, Litecoin, Polkadot, Polygon, Chainlink, Tron. Each page also works for any asset — just type your own prices.

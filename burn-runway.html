BUSINESS & FREELANCING CALCULATOR

Burn rate and runway calculator

Runway is how long your cash lasts at the current burn rate. Enter cash on hand, monthly revenue and monthly expenses to see the clock.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Net burn = Expenses − Revenue; Runway = Cash / Net burn

Worked example

  1. Cash $120,000, monthly revenue $5,000, monthly expenses $15,000.
  2. Net burn = $15,000 − $5,000 = $10,000 a month.
  3. Runway = 120000 / 10000 = 12 months.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Revenue and expenses stay flat; real startups rarely grow linearly.
  • No new funding is raised during the period.
  • Cash on hand is fully available to spend.

Frequently asked questions

Gross burn vs net burn?

Gross burn is total monthly spending; net burn subtracts revenue. Runway uses net burn because revenue offsets spending.

How much runway should a startup keep?

Twelve to eighteen months is a common target, so there is time to raise or reach profitability without a rushed deal.

Does runway change if revenue grows?

Yes. As revenue rises, net burn falls and runway extends — sometimes beyond the horizon shown here.

Present value calculator

Present value answers: what is a future sum worth today, once you account for the return it could earn?

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

PV = FV / (1 + r/n)^(n t)

Worked example

  1. FV = $20,000 in 10 years at a 7% discount rate.
  2. PV = 20000 / 1.07^10 = $10,166.99.
  3. With monthly compounding the figure is a little lower: 20000 / (1 + 0.07/12)^120 ≈ $9,952.

Assumptions and limitations

  • A constant discount rate.
  • The future amount is certain.
  • No tax on the return.

Frequently asked questions

What does the discount rate represent?

The return you could earn elsewhere, or the rate that reflects the risk of not receiving the money.

Why is present value lower than future value?

Money today can be invested, so a future sum must be discounted to compare it fairly.

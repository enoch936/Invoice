Sales tax calculator

Sales tax is charged on the final sale. Use this to add it to a price or to extract it from a receipt total.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Add: total = price × (1 + rate). Remove: price = total / (1 + rate)

Worked example

  1. A $100 item with 8% sales tax.
  2. Tax = 100 × 0.08 = $8.
  3. Total = $108; removing tax from $108 returns $100 pre-tax.

Assumptions and limitations

  • One tax rate applies to the whole sale.
  • No exemptions or multiple jurisdictions are combined.
  • Display rounding only.

Frequently asked questions

Why is the tax on a receipt not simply the rate times the total?

The total already includes tax, so the tax portion is total × rate / (1 + rate), not total × rate.

Can sales tax be compounded?

In some places state and local taxes stack, which can be modelled by adding the rates together here.

Do all goods get taxed?

No. Many jurisdictions exempt food, medicine or clothing, so apply this to taxable items only.

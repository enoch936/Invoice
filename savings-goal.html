Savings goal calculator

Enter the amount you are saving towards, what you already have and what you can put aside each month to see how long it will take.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Simulate each month: balance = balance × (1 + r/12) + deposit

Worked example

  1. Goal $20,000, already saved $2,000, deposit $300/month at 3%.
  2. Each month the balance earns 0.25% and grows by $300.
  3. The goal is reached in about 56 months (4 years 8 months).

Assumptions and limitations

  • The deposit and rate are constant.
  • Interest is credited monthly.
  • No tax or fees are applied.

Frequently asked questions

Why does time fall when the rate rises?

Higher interest adds more to the balance every month, so fewer deposits are needed to reach the target.

Does it assume I deposit on time?

Yes — it assumes every monthly deposit is made in full.

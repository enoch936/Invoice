Average calculator

The average, or arithmetic mean, adds up a set of numbers and divides by how many there are. Paste your values to get it at once.

Formula

mean = (x₁ + x₂ + … + xₙ) / n

Worked example

  1. Numbers: 10, 20, 30, 40, 50.
  2. Sum = 150 and the count is 5.
  3. Mean = 150 / 5 = 30; the median is also 30 and the range runs from 10 to 50.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Every value carries the same weight — this is the simple arithmetic mean.
  • The standard SQL-style average ignores blank entries; unreadable tokens are skipped.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between mean and median?

The mean divides the total by the count and is pulled by outliers; the median is the middle value and resists them.

Can the average be outside the range of the numbers?

No. The arithmetic mean always lies between the smallest and largest values.

How should I separate my numbers?

Commas, spaces, semicolons or new lines all work, so you can paste straight from a spreadsheet column.

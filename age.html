Age calculator (exact age from a birth date)

Enter a birth date to see an exact age in years, months and days, along with totals and the next birthday.

Formula

Count whole years, then whole months, then remaining days between the two dates.

Worked example

  1. Born 1990-06-15, aged on 2026-10-14.
  2. 36 years and 3 months have passed, plus 29 days.
  3. Total days alive: 13,269; next birthday in 244 days.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Whole-day arithmetic; the time of day is ignored.
  • The as-of date defaults to a fixed date provided with the page.
  • Leap days are handled by the calendar, not by an averaging formula.

Frequently asked questions

Why months and days as well as years?

Because ‘34 years’ hides how close someone is to 35. Exact years, months and days is what official forms usually ask for.

How is the next birthday counted?

It is the number of whole days from the as-of date to the next occurrence of the birth month and day.

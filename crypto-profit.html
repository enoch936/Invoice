TRADING, FOREX & CRYPTO CALCULATOR

Crypto profit calculator

Enter your entry and exit prices, quantity and fees to see the gross move, the fees you pay on both sides and what is really left.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Net P&L = (exit − entry) × quantity − (entry + exit) × quantity × fee% (sign flips for shorts)

Worked example

  1. Long 0.5 BTC: entry $60,000, exit $66,000, round-trip fee 0.1%.
  2. Gross = (66,000 − 60,000) × 0.5 = $3,000.
  3. Fees = (60,000 + 66,000) × 0.5 × 0.1% = $63, so the net is $2,937 — a 9.79% return on the $30,000 cost.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The whole quantity is opened and closed at the two prices given.
  • The fee is a round-trip (both sides) taker fee on the traded value; maker rebates and tiered fee schedules are not modelled.
  • Funding, slippage, taxes and price impact are excluded, and the trade is assumed to be fully filled.

Frequently asked questions

Why subtract fees twice?

A round trip opens and closes a position, and most exchanges charge on both. The fee percentage here applies to the entry and exit values together.

Can a short profit when the price rises?

No. A short gains when the price falls and loses when it rises, which is why the formula swaps entry and exit.

Is this a forecast?

No. It is arithmetic on the prices you enter. It does not predict the market and no outcome is guaranteed.

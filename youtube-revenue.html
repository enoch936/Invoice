WEBSITE & CREATOR INCOME CALCULATOR

YouTube revenue calculator

YouTube income comes from ad revenue and sponsorships. Enter your views, RPM and deal fees to see the combined monthly total.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Total = (Monetized views / 1,000) × RPM + Deals × Fee

Worked example

  1. 500,000 monetized views in a month.
  2. RPM is $4 per 1,000 monetized views.
  3. Ad revenue = (500,000 / 1,000) × $4 = $2,000.
  4. Two sponsorship deals at $1,500 each add $3,000, giving a $5,000 month.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The RPM is a blended estimate — real YouTube RPMs vary widely by niche, audience country, season and ad demand.
  • Sponsorship fees and deal counts are treated as flat monthly averages; real campaigns are irregular.
  • Figures are estimates only and ignore platform revenue shares, taxes and production costs; no income is guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between CPM and RPM on YouTube?

CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is what the creator keeps per 1,000 monetized views after YouTube's share.

Why is my RPM different each month?

Advertiser budgets, seasonality and the countries your viewers come from all move RPM up and down.

Do all views earn ad revenue?

No. Only monetized views — those that served an ad — count, and not every viewer sees or watches an ad.

Convert British pound (GBP) to euro (EUR)

The British pound (£) is the currency of the United Kingdom, issued under the authority of the Bank of England. The euro (€) is used in the euro area and is managed by the European Central Bank. To convert, multiply the GBP amount by the current GBP/EUR rate. The table above shows common amounts at the latest rate we could load.

If you invoice clients in EUR but think in GBP, convert the total on the day you send the invoice and state the rate used. Prices quoted in one currency and paid in another can shift between invoice date and payment date, so many freelancers add a short validity period or a currency-adjustment clause.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert GBP to EUR?

Multiply the GBP amount by the GBP to EUR exchange rate. Enter the amount above and the page does it using the latest rate it can load.

What is the GBP to EUR rate today?

The page loads an indicative mid-market rate when it can and shows it above the result. Rates change throughout the day.

Why is my bank's GBP to EUR rate different?

Banks and transfer services add a margin to the mid-market rate and may charge fixed fees, so the amount you receive is usually lower than a pure conversion.

Can I use my own GBP/EUR rate?

Yes. Type a rate in the manual override box and the result uses it instead.

Which currency should I invoice in, GBP or EUR?

Invoice in the currency your client pays in and your costs are in, if you can. If they differ, state the exchange rate and date on the invoice.

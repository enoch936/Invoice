Loan payoff calculator

Adding a little to every payment attacks the principal directly. Compare the scheduled payoff with a plan that pays extra.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Same amortisation, with payment = PMT + extra applied entirely to principal

Worked example

  1. $20,000 at 8% over 5 years, paying $100 extra a month.
  2. Scheduled payment is $405.53; paying $505.53 shortens the term by about 8 months.
  3. Total interest falls by roughly $500.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The lender applies extra payments straight to principal with no penalty.
  • The rate is fixed.
  • No prepayment fees.

Frequently asked questions

Should I pay extra or invest?

Compare the loan rate with the after-tax return you expect. Paying a 20% card is a guaranteed 20% saving; a 3% mortgage is often beaten elsewhere.

Does the extra go to interest first?

With most lenders an explicitly-labelled principal payment goes to principal, which is what this calculator assumes.

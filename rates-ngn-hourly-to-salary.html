How to convert an hourly rate to a salary

Annual pay = hourly rate × hours per week × paid weeks per year. Monthly pay is the annual figure divided by 12. For a Nigerian naira rate of 25 on a standard 40-hour, 52-week year, that is 25 × 40 × 52 = 52,000 per year.

Freelancers: the figure above assumes every hour is paid. Most independent workers can bill only 50–70% of their working hours, and have no paid leave, so a freelance hourly rate usually needs to be higher than the equivalent employee rate. Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an hourly rate in NGN to an annual salary?

Multiply the hourly rate by hours worked per week and by paid weeks per year. At 40 hours and 52 weeks, multiply the hourly rate by 2,080.

How many working hours are in a year?

A standard full-time year of 40 hours a week for 52 weeks is 2,080 hours. Paid leave and public holidays change the real figure.

Is the NGN annual figure before or after tax?

Before tax. Taxes, social contributions and benefits depend on where you live, so check local rules.

Should a freelancer charge the same hourly rate as an employee?

Usually more, because freelancers pay for their own tax, equipment and unpaid time between projects, and cannot bill every working hour.

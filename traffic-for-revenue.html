Traffic needed for a revenue goal calculator

Working backwards from a revenue target, see the traffic you would need at your current RPM.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Sessions = (Revenue goal / RPM) × 1,000

Worked example

  1. Revenue goal is $5,000 per month.
  2. Session RPM is $15 per 1,000 sessions.
  3. Sessions = (5,000 / 15) × 1,000 = 333,333 per month.
  4. That is about 11,111 sessions a day, or 500,000 pageviews at 1.5 pages per session.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The RPM is assumed to hold as traffic grows — in practice RPM often changes with audience mix, season and ad demand.
  • It assumes a 30-day month and a steady daily traffic pattern.
  • Results are estimates only; no income is guaranteed and network deductions are ignored.

Frequently asked questions

Why divide by 30?

Monthly traffic divided by roughly 30 days gives the average daily sessions needed, assuming even traffic throughout the month.

What if my RPM changes with scale?

Growth often changes RPM, so treat this as a snapshot. Re-run it with a conservative RPM to plan more safely.

Does this include all revenue sources?

Only if the RPM you enter reflects all of them. Ad, affiliate and sponsorship income can be blended into one RPM.

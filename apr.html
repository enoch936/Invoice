APR calculator

The interest rate ignores fees; the APR folds them in, so it is the honest number to compare loans by.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

APR is the rate r where PV(payments at r) = amount − fees. Solved numerically.

Worked example

  1. Borrow $20,000 at 7% over 5 years with $500 fees.
  2. Payment = $396.02; you actually receive $19,500.
  3. The rate that equates those payments to $19,500 is about 7.6% APR.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fees are paid upfront and reduce the amount you receive.
  • No ongoing fees.
  • Solved by bisection to high precision.

Frequently asked questions

Why is APR higher than the interest rate?

Fees add cost without adding borrowing power, so the effective annual rate rises.

Is APR the same everywhere?

No — jurisdictions define APR differently and some include insurance or compulsory charges.

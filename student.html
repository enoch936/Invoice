How it works

GPA = Σ(credits × grade points) ÷ Σ credits. This uses a common 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, A− = 3.7, B+ = 3.3 and so on). Schools differ, so check your own scale.

Required GPA = (target × total credits − current GPA × credits completed) ÷ credits remaining.

Frequently asked questions

How is GPA calculated?

Multiply each course's grade points by its credits, add the results, and divide by total credits. A 3-credit A and a 3-credit B give (3×4 + 3×3) ÷ 6 = 3.5.

What GPA do I need to reach my target?

Use the grade planner: enter your current GPA, credits completed, credits remaining and your target. It returns the average GPA you need across the remaining credits.

Does this work for a 5.0 or weighted scale?

The calculator uses a standard 4.0 scale. Weighted or percentage systems use different grade points, so results will not match those schools.

Is my data saved anywhere?

Your courses are stored only in this browser's local storage. Use the Clear my data link in the footer to delete them.

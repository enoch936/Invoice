Convert euro (EUR) to Kenyan shilling (KES)

The euro (€) is the currency of the euro area, issued under the authority of the European Central Bank. The Kenyan shilling (KSh) is used in Kenya and is managed by the Central Bank of Kenya. To convert, multiply the EUR amount by the current EUR/KES rate. The table above shows common amounts at the latest rate we could load.

If you invoice clients in KES but think in EUR, convert the total on the day you send the invoice and state the rate used. Prices quoted in one currency and paid in another can shift between invoice date and payment date, so many freelancers add a short validity period or a currency-adjustment clause.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert EUR to KES?

Multiply the EUR amount by the EUR to KES exchange rate. Enter the amount above and the page does it using the latest rate it can load.

What is the EUR to KES rate today?

The page loads an indicative mid-market rate when it can and shows it above the result. Rates change throughout the day.

Why is my bank's EUR to KES rate different?

Banks and transfer services add a margin to the mid-market rate and may charge fixed fees, so the amount you receive is usually lower than a pure conversion.

Can I use my own EUR/KES rate?

Yes. Type a rate in the manual override box and the result uses it instead.

Which currency should I invoice in, EUR or KES?

Invoice in the currency your client pays in and your costs are in, if you can. If they differ, state the exchange rate and date on the invoice.

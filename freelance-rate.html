BUSINESS & FREELANCING CALCULATOR

Freelance hourly rate calculator

Freelancers only bill part of their working time. This works backwards from the income you want to find the rate you must charge.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Rate = [Take-home / (1 − tax%) + Expenses] / (Hours per week × Weeks per year)

Worked example

  1. Target $80,000 take-home, $10,000 expenses, 25% tax, 25 billable hours a week for 48 weeks.
  2. Gross before tax = 80000 / 0.75 = $106,667; add expenses = $116,667.
  3. Billable hours = 1,200, so the rate is 116667 / 1200 = $97.22 an hour.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Only billable hours are counted — admin and marketing time is unpaid.
  • The tax rate is an average effective rate, not a marginal one.
  • Expenses are annual and fully deductible.

Frequently asked questions

Why is this higher than a salary equivalent?

A salaried employee is paid for all hours, gets paid holidays and has employer taxes paid for them — freelancers must build all of that into the rate.

What counts as a billable hour?

Only hours you can actually charge a client. Admin, invoicing, marketing and holidays are not billable.

Should I include a profit margin?

Yes, ideally. Add a buffer to the expenses or target so the business funds itself rather than just paying you.

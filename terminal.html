What this terminal does

The terminal renders real exchange candles streamed over a WebSocket and calculates every study from that data with the same pure engine used across the site. It is a workspace, not a broker: there is no order routing and no account connection.

Coverage, stated honestly. Crypto spot instruments from Binance are real-time with full OHLCV history. Binance's fiat markets (EUR/USDT, USDT/TRY, USDT/BRL and so on) stream over the same real-time WebSocket, quoted in a USD-pegged stablecoin rather than interbank rates. True forex pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY and crosses) work when you add a free Twelve Data key in Settings: real OHLC, but polled every 15 seconds and therefore labelled DELAYED, not LIVE. Stocks, ETFs, indices, commodities and bond yields are not connected — they need a keyed data vendor or a server-side proxy that a static GitHub Pages site cannot safely keep a secret for, so they are never simulated.

Frequently asked questions

What markets does the terminal cover?

Crypto and fiat spot markets from Binance stream in real time with full candle history. True forex pairs are fetched on a 15-second timer from Twelve Data when you supply your own free key and are labelled DELAYED. Stocks, indices, commodities and bond yields are not wired because they require a keyed provider or a server-side proxy, which a static GitHub Pages site cannot safely hold.

Can I get a live forex (EUR/USD) chart?

Yes, with a caveat. Add a free Twelve Data API key under Settings → Forex data and open any pair from the symbol search. You get real forex candles and indicators, refreshed every 15 seconds. Because that free tier is polled rather than streamed, the status badge reads DELAYED, not LIVE. Binance's fiat markets (EUR/USDT and similar) do stream in real time, but they are quoted in a USD stablecoin, not interbank FX.

Is the data live or delayed?

Crypto and Binance fiat markets are genuinely real-time over a WebSocket and the status badge reads LIVE. Everything else is labelled by its true state: DELAYED for polled forex and the daily FX reference rates, CACHED for historical candles without a stream, and ERROR when a feed is unreachable. Nothing is ever labelled real-time unless it is streamed.

Are the indicators and chart types calculated or faked?

Every indicator is calculated from the loaded candles by the shared js/quant.js engine, and derived chart types such as Heikin-Ashi and Renko are computed from the source candles with documented formulas and labelled as derived. The terminal never generates substitute prices.

Does bar replay leak future data?

No. During replay the engine and every indicator are computed only from candles up to the replay cursor, so future candles and future indicator values are never visible.

Where are my settings and drawings stored?

Entirely in your browser using versioned local storage. They are validated on read, never uploaded, and can be exported and imported as JSON.

Can I trade from this page?

No. Alerts run only while the page is open and the browser allows notifications. There is no order routing and no broker connection.

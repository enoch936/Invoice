Credit card payoff calculator

Credit cards charge interest on the balance you carry. Enter the balance, APR and what you can pay each month to see the payoff time and true cost.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Each month: balance = balance × (1 + APR/12) − payment

Worked example

  1. $4,000 at 22% APR paying $150 a month.
  2. The monthly rate is 22/12 = 1.833% and interest starts at $73.33.
  3. The balance clears in about 34 months with roughly $1,048 of interest.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The APR and payment stay constant.
  • No new spending is added to the card.
  • Payments are made at the end of each month.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a small payment take so long?

When the payment barely exceeds the monthly interest, almost nothing reduces the balance, so the term stretches out.

Does the minimum payment clear the card?

Minimum payments are often 1–3% of the balance and can take years. Use a fixed payment here to see the difference.

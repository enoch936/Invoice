WEBSITE & CREATOR INCOME CALCULATOR

Website profit calculator

Revenue is not profit. Subtract your running costs to see what your site really earns, and the RPM needed to break even.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Profit = Revenue − (Hosting + Tools + Content + Other) · Margin = Profit / Revenue × 100

Worked example

  1. Revenue is $4,000 a month.
  2. Costs are $20 hosting + $100 tools + $300 content + $50 other = $470.
  3. Profit = $4,000 − $470 = $3,530, a margin of 88.25%.
  4. At 100,000 pageviews the break-even RPM is (470 / 100,000) × 1,000 = $4.70.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Costs are the monthly running amounts you enter; one-off or annual costs should be divided into a monthly figure.
  • Revenue is an estimate from your own inputs and will vary with CPM/RPM, traffic and season. No income is guaranteed.
  • Taxes, your own time and capital spending are not included unless you add them to 'other costs'.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a tool cost?

Any recurring software subscription used to run the site, such as analytics, SEO, email or design software.

What does break-even RPM mean?

It is the revenue per 1,000 pageviews you must earn just to cover your costs — below it the site loses money.

Should I pay myself a salary?

If the site is your job, include a fair wage in costs; otherwise the 'profit' overstates the true return on your time.

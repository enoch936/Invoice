CAGR calculator (compound annual growth rate)

CAGR smooths an investment's growth into a single yearly rate, ignoring the bumps along the way.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

CAGR = (Ending / Beginning)^(1 / years) − 1

Worked example

  1. Start at $10,000, end at $18,000 after 5 years.
  2. Ratio = 1.8; CAGR = 1.8^(1/5) − 1 = 12.47% a year.
  3. Check: 10000 × 1.1247^5 ≈ $18,000, so the rate reconciles with the endpoints.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Only the two endpoints matter — volatility in between is ignored.
  • No contributions or withdrawals.
  • No tax or fees.

Frequently asked questions

Is CAGR the actual yearly return?

No. It is the smoothed rate that would take you from the start to the end. Real years vary around it.

Can CAGR be negative?

Yes, if the ending value is below the beginning value the CAGR is negative.

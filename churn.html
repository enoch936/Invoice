Churn rate calculator

Churn is the share of customers who leave in a period. It drives growth, lifetime value and how hard you must work to stand still.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Churn % = Customers lost / Customers at start × 100

Worked example

  1. Start the month with 1,000 customers and lose 40.
  2. Churn = 40 / 1000 = 4%.
  3. Retention = 100% − 4% = 96% for the month.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Lost customers are counted once in the period.
  • New customers are excluded from the base.
  • The measure is customer churn, not revenue churn.

Frequently asked questions

Customer churn vs revenue churn?

Customer churn counts heads lost; revenue churn measures the money lost, which differs when customers pay different amounts.

How do I annualise churn?

Monthly churn compounds: 1 − (1 − monthly)^12. A 4% monthly churn is roughly 39% a year.

Why does churn matter so much?

It caps growth and shrinks customer lifetime value, so a leaking bucket needs ever more acquisition to stand still.

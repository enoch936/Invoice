Percentage calculator

A percentage is a fraction out of one hundred. Pick the question you are asking and enter two numbers to get the answer with the working shown.

Formula

part = whole × percent/100 · percent = part/whole × 100 · whole = part / (percent/100)

Worked example

  1. A percent of a number: 25% of 80 → 25/100 = 0.25 and 0.25 × 80 = 20.
  2. What percent: 15 of 60 → 15 / 60 = 0.25, which is 25%.
  3. The whole: 12 is 30% of what → 30/100 = 0.3 and 12 / 0.3 = 40.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Percentages are treated as exact proportions out of one hundred.
  • The values can be any real numbers, including amounts, scores or counts.

Frequently asked questions

What is the quick way to take a percent of something?

Move the decimal point two places to turn the percent into a fraction (25% becomes 0.25), then multiply by the whole.

Why can B not be zero?

Expressing A as a percent of B means dividing by B, and dividing by zero is undefined.

Is a 50% rise the same as a 50% fall?

No. Rising 100 to 150 is +50%, but falling back 150 to 100 is only about −33.3% because the base changed.

MRR and ARR calculator

MRR and ARR are the standard gauges of subscription revenue. They scale a per-customer figure up to the whole base.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

MRR = Customers × ARPU; ARR = MRR × 12

Worked example

  1. 500 paying customers at $49 each a month.
  2. MRR = 500 × $49 = $24,500.
  3. ARR = $24,500 × 12 = $294,000.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Every customer pays the same average amount.
  • The base is stable — no new or churned customers in the period.
  • All revenue is genuinely recurring.

Frequently asked questions

MRR or ARR — which should I track?

Use MRR for month-to-month decisions and ARR when comparing annual contracts or talking to investors.

Does ARR just multiply MRR by 12?

For simple reporting, yes. Strictly it should be the annualised value of contracted recurring revenue.

How does churn interact with MRR?

Net revenue retention nets new and expanded revenue against churn, showing whether the base grows on its own.

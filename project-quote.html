Project quote calculator

A good quote prices the work, cushions the estimate and shows any discount clearly. Enter the hours and rate to see the final total.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Total = (Hours × Rate) × (1 + Contingency%) × (1 − Discount%)

Worked example

  1. 40 hours at $85 an hour is a $3,400 base.
  2. A 10% contingency adds $340, giving a $3,740 subtotal.
  3. A 5% discount takes off $187, so the quote is $3,553 (about $88.83 an hour).

Assumptions and limitations

  • Hours is a realistic estimate of the work.
  • Contingency is a fixed percentage of the base, not of the discounted total.
  • Discount is applied after the contingency is added.

Frequently asked questions

Why add a contingency?

Projects rarely run exactly to estimate. A buffer absorbs small overruns without needing an awkward conversation or a change request.

Should the discount come before or after contingency?

Applying it after the contingency (as here) treats the buffer as part of the price you are discounting from — decide deliberately.

When should I quote a fixed price?

When the scope is clear and you can absorb the risk. Use an hourly rate for exploratory or changing work.

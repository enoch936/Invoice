CPM calculator (cost per 1,000 impressions)

CPM is the price paid for 1,000 ad impressions. Enter your impressions and rate to estimate the revenue they generate.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Revenue = (Impressions / 1,000) × CPM

Worked example

  1. A page shows 100,000 ad impressions in a month.
  2. The agreed CPM is $5 per 1,000 impressions.
  3. Revenue = (100,000 / 1,000) × $5 = $500.
  4. Each single impression is therefore worth about $0.005.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Revenue is an estimate based only on the impressions and CPM you enter — real CPMs vary by traffic, season and advertiser demand.
  • It assumes every impression is a paid, billable impression with no unfilled or invalid traffic.
  • No income is guaranteed; ad networks may apply deductions, viewability rules or invalid-traffic clawbacks.

Frequently asked questions

What does CPM stand for?

Cost per mille — 'mille' is Latin for thousand, so CPM is the cost (or revenue) for 1,000 impressions.

Is CPM the same as RPM?

No. CPM is what an advertiser pays per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is revenue per 1,000 pageviews or sessions and includes all sources.

Why is my real revenue lower than this figure?

Unfilled ad requests, invalid traffic filtering and network revenue shares all reduce the amount you actually receive.

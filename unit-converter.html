CONVERTERS, DATES & HEALTH CALCULATOR

Unit converter (length, mass, temperature, volume, area, speed, time, data)

Convert a value from one unit to another across eight common categories, using exact standard conversion factors.

Formula

result = value × (factor_from / factor_to), with temperature handled by dedicated formulas

Worked example

  1. Convert 1 metre to feet: 1 × 1 / 0.3048 = 3.2808 ft.
  2. Convert 70 kg to pounds: 70 × 1 / 0.45359237 = 154.32 lb.
  3. Convert 100 °C to Fahrenheit: 100 × 9/5 + 32 = 212 °F.

Assumptions and limitations

  • US (not imperial/UK) liquid gallons and fluid ounces are used for volume.
  • Data units use binary multiples (1 KB = 1024 bytes).
  • Factors are exact defined values; the displayed result is rounded for readability.

Frequently asked questions

Why do I have to pick units from the same category?

Converting metres to kilograms has no meaning. The unit labels show their category so you can pick a matching pair.

Are the conversion factors exact?

Yes. The factors used (mile, foot, inch, pound, ounce, gallon and so on) are the international standard definitions, stored as exact decimals.

Why does a kilobyte show as 1024 bytes?

Digital storage is commonly reported in binary multiples (1 KB = 1024 B). Some vendors use 1000; this tool follows the 1024 convention and says so.

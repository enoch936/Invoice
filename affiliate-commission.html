Affiliate commission calculator

Affiliate income depends on how many orders you refer, their value and the commission rate. See the gross and net payout.

Estimates only. Results depend on the inputs you provide and the assumptions listed below. This is an educational tool, not financial, tax or investment advice.

Formula

Net = Orders × Order value × Commission% × (1 − Refund%)

Worked example

  1. 200 affiliate referrals convert in a month.
  2. The average order is $80 and the commission rate is 30%.
  3. Gross = 200 × 80 × 0.30 = $4,800.
  4. With 5% refunds, net = 4,800 × 0.95 = $4,560 a month.

Assumptions and limitations

  • The order value, commission rate and refund rate are treated as flat averages across the month.
  • Refunds are assumed to reverse the commission in full, which not every programme does.
  • Results depend on conversion and refund behaviour that will vary — this is an estimate and no income is guaranteed.

Frequently asked questions

Why deduct refunds?

Most affiliate programmes claw back commission when an order is refunded or charged back, so gross numbers overstate what you keep.

Are there recurring commissions?

Some programmes pay on renewals. Add those separately, since this calculator models a single upfront payout.

Should I use average or actual order value?

Use a realistic average. If a few large orders dominate, consider modelling them separately.
